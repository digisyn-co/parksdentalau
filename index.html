Welcome to Park St

Historic charm, contemporary dentistry.

Park St Dental Practice is a privately owned and operated modern dental practice, located at 26 Park Street, Mona Vale, within a beautifully preserved heritage-listed building — one of the oldest in the area.

For decades we've cared for generations of families across Mona Vale and the Northern Beaches. We believe dentistry is about more than just teeth — it's about people, trust, and long-term oral health. Led by our principal dentist, Dr Neda Gorji, our close-knit team of dentists, surgeons and support staff genuinely care about your comfort and wellbeing.

Our initial consultation fee is $90 (excluding X-rays), including a comprehensive assessment and a personalised treatment plan with clear, estimated costs — so you can make informed decisions about your care.

Good to Know

Every question, gently answered.

Choosing a dentist is personal. Here's what patients most often ask before their first visit.

How much does the first visit cost?

Our initial consultation fee is $90, excluding X-rays. It includes a comprehensive assessment of your oral health; any necessary X-rays and tests are at an additional cost. We'll then discuss our findings, provide a personalised treatment plan, and outline estimated costs. Conditions apply.

I'm a nervous patient — will I be okay?

You're in gentle hands. We're known for our care of nervous patients and children, and we offer sedation and sleep dentistry options so your visit is as calm and comfortable as possible.

Do you see children?

Absolutely — children's dentistry is one of our special interests. We've cared for generations of Northern Beaches families, and we love making a child's first dental experiences positive ones.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us on (02) 9997 5770. We provide emergency dentistry, and early mornings and late afternoons are available by appointment.

Is parking easy?

Yes — we're conveniently located at 26 Park Street, Mona Vale, with onsite parking available.
